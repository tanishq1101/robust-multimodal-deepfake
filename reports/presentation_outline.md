## Demo script (2 minutes)
1. Open the Gradio link (`notebooks/08_demo_and_report.ipynb` or `python app/app.py --share`).
2. Upload a real clip. Point out the verdict, the face and voice scores, and that heatmaps show evidence for REAL.
3. Upload a FakeVideo-RealAudio clip. The face model flags it and the heatmap sits on the face.
4. Upload a RealVideo-FakeAudio clip. The voice model flags it; show the spectrogram heatmap.
5. Close with the robustness slide: the same clip after heavy compression.
