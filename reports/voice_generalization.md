# Voice model outside its training domain

Checkpoint: `audio_asvspoof_3a53cf51` (trained on ASVspoof 2019 LA; in-domain test AUC 0.977, EER 6.2%).

Test set: 95 clips from the VITS paper's demo page, 10 genuine recordings and 85 synthetic. Generated by `scripts/eval_voice_generalization.py`.

## Result

| Scoring                               |   AUC | EER   | Real called real   | Fake called fake   |
|:--------------------------------------|------:|:------|:-------------------|:-------------------|
| mean over 4 s windows (as in the app) | 0.586 | 53.8% | 0 / 10             | 85 / 85            |
| centre 4 s window (as in training)    | 0.614 | 50.3% | 0 / 10             | 85 / 85            |

The model rates almost every clip as fake, genuine recordings included, so its ranking is close to chance on this data. ASVspoof 2019 LA's genuine speech comes from VCTK speakers, yet the genuine VCTK clips here score near 1.0: the model keys on recording and editing traits of the ASVspoof files (for example the silence around each utterance, see Müller et al., 2021) more than on the voice itself.

## What this means

- In the app, a FAKE that rests on the voice model alone comes with a caution note, and the video verdict can be switched to *face only*.
- A voice model for real-world use needs more varied genuine speech in training (other corpora, microphones and rooms), silence trimming so the model cannot use the silence shortcut, codec augmentation (`--robust-augment`), and an out-of-domain test set such as In-the-Wild (Müller et al., 2022).
